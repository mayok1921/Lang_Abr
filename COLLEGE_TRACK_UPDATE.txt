Major additions
---------------
• Japanese college sequence:
  Japanese I — JLPT N5
  Japanese II — JLPT N4
  Japanese III — JLPT N3
  Japanese IV — JLPT N2

• Polish sequence:
  Polish I — A1
  Polish II — A2
  Polish III — B1
  Polish IV — B2

• Mexican Spanish sequence:
  Mexican Spanish I — A1
  Mexican Spanish II — A2
  Mexican Spanish III — B1
  Mexican Spanish IV — B2

• Each course has:
  - six structured units
  - expected weekly workload
  - prerequisite
  - progress
  - practice accuracy
  - standing/grade-style indicator
  - native-first study flow
  - reading/context practice
  - midterm cumulative checkpoint
  - final cumulative checkpoint

• Added 400+ academic/course learning cards across the three languages.
• Existing skill library, slang, kana drawing, 85% drawing scoring, progress, glass UI and Home-only QR remain.

Important scope note
--------------------
This is a major curriculum expansion and gives Keon a real college-style structure, but a truly exhaustive JLPT N2 program eventually needs thousands of vocabulary items, roughly a thousand kanji-level targets, extensive listening audio, and many long reading passages. The architecture now supports continued expansion toward that depth without redesigning the app.
