Keon Japanese drawing accuracy update

Replace/upload these files in the root of Lang_Abr:
- index.html
- app.js
- service-worker.js

You can also upload the other files in this ZIP if you want the complete current Keon package.

Drawing behavior:
- Japanese writing questions now have Check Drawing.
- Drawing is normalized for size and position.
- A tolerance zone is used for finger wobble.
- 85%+ passes automatically.
- 70–84% says Close and lets the learner retry.
- Below 70% asks the learner to try again.
- Passing automatically records the answer as correct and advances.
- Trace guide remains available.

This is shape-based checking, not true handwritten stroke-order recognition.
