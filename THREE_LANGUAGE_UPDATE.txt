Keon three-language native-first update

Replace/upload:
- index.html
- app.js
- service-worker.js

What changed
------------
1. Added Mexican Spanish as a full third language.
2. Japanese, Polish, and Mexican Spanish now use the same native-first model:
   - target-language text first
   - learner tries to read/understand it
   - tap to reveal pronunciation/reading when useful
   - reveal English meaning only after trying
   - reveal a natural example/context
3. Added natural/slang paths:
   - Japanese: yabai, maji de, meccha, etc.
   - Polish: spoko, serio?, nie ogarniam, etc.
   - Mexican Spanish: qué onda, no manches, órale, güey, ni modo, etc.
4. Mexican Spanish is intentionally Mexico-focused rather than mixing regional Spanish varieties.
5. Added Mexican Spanish placement test, recommendations, context/input decks, progress tracking, and home-screen choices.
6. Existing Japanese kana drawing and 85% drawing check remain.
7. QR remains on the main Home screen only.

Learning principle
------------------
Keon should not train constant word-for-word translation. English is still available so the learner is not confused, but only after an attempt to recognize/read the target language.
